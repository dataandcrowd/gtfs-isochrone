Notes on the scenarios/ files
-----------------------------
- 1a, 1c, 2c come from the official Auckland Council TOUC shortlist maps.
- 3b, 3c, 3e are derived from the state-highway network clipped to a
  city-centre buffered polygon supplied by the modelling team.
  The v4 build closed the 979 m Auckland Harbour Bridge gap in the SH1
  linework by adding one straight segment plus the Auckland-Waiwera
  Motorway record.

Notes on the roads/ files
-------------------------
- auckland_state_highways.gpkg    twelve unclipped SH lines.
- sh_clipped_with_bridge.gpkg     eight lines used to build 3b/3c/3e.

Notes on outputs/ cleanup (2026-07-18)
---------------------------------------
- The four sa2_*.gpkg pipeline-intermediate files (sa2_prepared,
  sa2_accessibility, sa2_equity, sa2_final) were moved from outputs/ into
  data/sa2/, since each is read as input by a later stage script. All
  stage1-5 and concept_fig_* scripts were updated to read/write them from
  data/sa2/ instead of outputs/.
- Ten stale result files generated by the old stage4b/stage4c pipeline
  before the 2c double-cordon bug fix (od_classification.gpkg,
  od_equity_summary.csv, od_burden_crosstab.csv, od_morans_i.csv,
  od_lisa_summary.csv, od_charged_by_scenario.csv, scenario_boundaries.gpkg,
  scenario_boundaries_summary.csv, equity_summary.csv, burden_crosstab.csv)
  were deleted as superseded by outputs/equity_summary_final.csv (from the
  corrected rerun_analysis.py pipeline).
- Nine unreferenced junk/debug files were also deleted.
- code/_plot_appendix_maps.py, _plot_fig_4_2a_bars.py, and
  _plot_fig_4_2b_lisa_maps.py were rewired to the corrected rerun_v2
  outputs and now run cleanly:
    - fig_4_2a_bars.py reads outputs/equity_summary_final.csv
    - fig_4_2b_lisa_maps.py and _plot_appendix_maps.py read
      outputs/burden_by_sa2_final.gpkg (504-SA2 layer with per-scenario
      lisa_{scenario} columns, copied in from the rerun_analysis.py
      pipeline) instead of the deleted od_classification.gpkg
    - the old accessibility-cluster auto-zoom (lisa_access45 == "HH")
      was replaced with a fixed NZTM extent covering the 504-SA2 urban
      study area, since that column no longer exists in the corrected
      pipeline's output
